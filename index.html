Copilot Training
Modules

Copilot can see everything you can. Train for that.

Six short modules on using Microsoft 365 Copilot well — and using it safely in a workplace where protected health information is part of daily work. Work through them in order. Each one ends with a short quiz.

1 · Read

Work through the module page. The amber flags are the rules that protect PHI.

2 · Practice

Follow the linked Microsoft Learn lessons — hands-on, at your own pace.

3 · Pass the quiz

Each module ends with a short quiz. Completions are recorded automatically.

  • M-01

    Copilot Fundamentals

    What Copilot is, what data it can reach, and why it only ever sees what you already have permission to see.

    ≈ 30 min · start here
  • M-02

    Copilot in Word & Outlook

    Drafting, rewriting, and summarizing documents; catching up on and replying to email threads.

    ≈ 30 min
  • M-03

    Copilot in Excel & Teams

    Analyzing data, building formulas, meeting recaps, and catching up on channels you missed.

    ≈ 30 min
  • M-04

    Copilot Chat

    Writing prompts that work, work grounding vs. web grounding, and verifying what Copilot tells you.

    ≈ 25 min
  • M-05

    PHI & AI Rules

    The module that matters most here: minimum necessary in the age of AI, what never goes in a prompt, and what to do when something goes wrong.

    ≈ 40 min · required for all staff
  • M-06

    HIPAA Refresher

    Your training obligations under the Privacy and Security Rules, the 18 identifiers, and breach basics.

    ≈ 35 min · required for all staff

This site contains general training material only. It holds no patient information and no internal system details. Quiz responses are collected in Microsoft Forms inside our Microsoft 365 tenant — never on this site. Questions about a rule? Ask the Privacy Officer before you act, not after.
