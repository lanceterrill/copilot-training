Copilot Fundamentals

≈ 30 minutes · no prior AI experience needed

What Copilot is

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant built into the apps you already use — Word, Outlook, Excel, Teams, and a standalone chat. You ask for something in plain language; it drafts, summarizes, analyzes, or answers. It is a very capable assistant and a sometimes-confident one: it can be wrong, and it is your job to check its work before that work leaves your hands.

What Copilot can see — and why that matters here

Copilot answers work questions by "grounding" itself in your organization's Microsoft 365 data: your email, your files, your meetings, your chats. The key rule is simple:

  • Copilot can only access content you personally already have permission to open.
  • It never gains extra access — but it makes everything you can access dramatically easier to find and summarize.
  • Your prompts and Copilot's responses stay within our Microsoft 365 tenant and are not used to train the underlying AI models.
PHI rule

Because Copilot can instantly surface anything you can open, every over-broad file permission becomes an over-broad AI answer. If Copilot ever shows you patient information you don't believe you should have access to, stop, don't share it, and report it the same day. That is a permissions problem, and finding it is a favor to everyone.
