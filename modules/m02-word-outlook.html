M-02

Copilot in Word & Outlook

≈ 30 minutes · complete M-01 first

Word: drafting and rewriting

  • Draft with Copilot — describe the document you need ("a one-page summary of the attached policy for new hires") and refine from there.
  • Rewrite — select any text and ask for a different tone, length, or reading level.
  • Summarize — open a long document and ask for key points, decisions, or action items.
  • Reference other files you have access to by typing / and the file name — the draft grounds itself in that content.

Outlook: taming email

  • Summarize a thread — one click condenses a 30-reply thread to what was decided and what's still open.
  • Draft a reply — give Copilot the intent ("decline politely, propose next week") and edit the result.
  • Coaching — ask Copilot to review a draft you wrote for tone and clarity before sending.
PHI rule

A Copilot draft is your document the moment you hit send. Read every AI-drafted email and letter completely before it leaves — especially the recipient line and any names, dates, or details Copilot pulled in from other messages. Auto-summarized threads can carry patient details into contexts where they don't belong. Minimum necessary applies to what you forward, not just what you write.
