M-03

Copilot in Excel & Teams

≈ 30 minutes · complete M-01 first

Excel: analysis without formula wrestling

  • Ask about your data — "which month had the highest volume?" "show trends by department" — Copilot works best on data formatted as a table.
  • Formula help — describe the calculation you want; Copilot proposes the formula column and explains it.
  • Highlight and visualize — ask for conditional formatting or a suggested chart.
  • Verify the numbers. Spot-check a few of Copilot's calculations by hand before anything goes into a report.

Teams: meetings and channels

  • Meeting recap — for recorded/transcribed meetings, Copilot produces summaries, decisions, and action items, and can answer "what did I miss?" if you join late.
  • Chat catch-up — ask Copilot to summarize a channel or chat since you last looked.
  • During the meeting — ask for open questions or points of disagreement so far.
PHI rule

Transcription turns spoken patient details into searchable, summarizable text. Before recording or transcribing any meeting where patients may be discussed, confirm it's permitted for that meeting type and that everyone present knows. And remember the spreadsheet rule that predates AI: a "de-identified" sheet with dates of service, ZIP codes, and rare diagnoses often isn't de-identified at all — M-06 covers the 18 identifiers that must be gone.

Learn it hands-on

  • Get started with Microsoft 365 Copilot — apps in action learn.microsoft.com · includes Excel and Teams walkthroughs
  • Copilot use-case exercises by role learn.microsoft.com · Finance, Operations, HR tracks use Excel heavily

Before you take the quiz, you should be able to

  • Ask Copilot a question about a table in Excel and spot-check its answer.
  • Generate and act on a Teams meeting recap.
  • State when a meeting may be recorded or transcribed, and who must know.
